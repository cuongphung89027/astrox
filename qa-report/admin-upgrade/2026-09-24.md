## Data definitions and limits

- Sessions are anonymous, tab-scoped 30-minute browser activity sessions, not identified users or cross-device people.
- Feature events start with this release. Missing prior coverage is null, never manufactured historical zero. Partial observed periods are labeled; incomplete comparisons are withheld.
- Client-reported signals can be absent or forged. They never authorize login, Point credits or payment. DNT is respected; payloads exclude birth details, questions, prompts, readings and account IDs.
- Starts cover AI requests, Tarot drawing, Kinh Dich rituals and Western compatibility calculation. Four profile-driven automatic calculators have route and AI/cache-result coverage but no fabricated explicit calculation-start action.
- Completion rates and per-feature error rates are not inferred from unrelated aggregate events. Authoritative AI completion/failure metrics are in the existing AI report.
- Per-user login history cannot be reconstructed from diagnostics without a user identifier; timeline shows available sync/service/reward/payment records.
- Collector prunes records older than 90 days upon incoming events; no scheduled retention job is introduced.
- Full-period aggregates were tested beyond 5,000 AI requests/events; server user search was tested beyond 200 users. These are correctness tests, not a production load benchmark.
- Cross-browser Safari/Firefox and a physical mobile device were not independently tested. Responsive and interaction checks used the Chromium in-app browser.
